About BRIMS

A credible place to buy or swap cars

BRIMS Autos is a car dealership based on Shehu Yar'adua Way, Mabushi, Abuja. We deal in brand new, foreign used and locally used cars — and we're specialists in car swaps.

Run by a team of young, vibrant minds, we're known for polite staff, honest pricing, and the expertise to buy, sell, swap or transform your car in a jiffy.
